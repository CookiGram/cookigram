# Prompt d’illustration — Rice bowl au porc haché, gochujang et soja

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: horizontal 16:9 hero illustration for a responsive recipe website
Primary request: Create an entirely original CookiGram manga-inspired culinary illustration based only on the written recipe, not on any existing photograph.
Scene/backdrop: A simple warm home-kitchen tabletop, softly suggested and uncluttered.
Subject: One simple ceramic bowl of fluffy separate white long-grain rice topped with glossy, deeply browned and lightly caramelized ground pork crumbles coated in red-brown gochujang and soy glaze. Show fine onion pieces and garnish only with finely sliced green onions.
Style/medium: Contemporary manga food illustration with elegant fine ink contours, hand-painted watercolor texture and soft cel shading; believable appetizing food, semi-realistic illustration, generic original CookiGram visual style, without imitating any named artist, studio, series or existing artwork.
Composition/framing: Landscape 16:9, three-quarter view at about 45 degrees, bowl centred with generous safe crop margins for mobile and desktop cards.
Lighting/mood: Warm natural kitchen light, welcoming and homely.
Colour palette: White rice, glossy red-brown pork, fresh green onion and warm wood tones.
Constraints: Faithful to the recipe; original composition.
Avoid: Egg, sesame seeds, avocado, cucumber, kimchi or other unsupported toppings; text, lettering, logos, brand marks, watermarks, product packaging, people, hands, faces, third-party image imitation and photorealism.
