# Prompt d’illustration — Ratatouille

Create an entirely original horizontal 16:9 culinary illustration for CookiGram, based only on the written recipe. Show a rustic shallow casserole of ratatouille with clearly recognizable aubergine, courgette, tomato, onion and red/yellow pepper pieces, glossy with a light coating of olive oil and softly stewed rather than puréed. Warm contemporary manga-inspired food illustration, fine ink contours, hand-painted watercolor texture and soft cel shading. Three-quarter view, centered dish, simple warm home-kitchen tabletop, generous safe margins for responsive crops. No unsupported garnish, no text, no logo, no watermark, no packaging, no people, no photorealism.
