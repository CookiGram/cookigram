# Prompt d’illustration — Hachis parmentier

Create an entirely original horizontal 16:9 culinary illustration for CookiGram, based only on the written recipe. Show a family-size gratin dish of hachis parmentier with a deeply golden gruyère crust, ridged creamy mashed-potato topping and a visible cut portion revealing a moist minced-beef, carrot, onion and shallot layer underneath. Warm contemporary manga-inspired food illustration, fine ink contours, hand-painted watercolor texture and soft cel shading. Three-quarter view, centered dish on a simple home-kitchen table, appetizing steam, safe margins for mobile crops. Keep only the gratin as the subject; no unsupported herbs, garnish, loose ingredients or decorative props. No text, logo, watermark, packaging, people or photorealism.
