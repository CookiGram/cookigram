# Prompt d’illustration — Bœuf Stroganoff à la poêle

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: horizontal 16:9 hero illustration for a responsive recipe website
Primary request: Create an entirely original manga-inspired culinary illustration of "Bœuf Stroganoff à la poêle" based only on this written dish description, not on any existing photograph.
Scene/backdrop: A simple warm home-kitchen tabletop, softly suggested and uncluttered.
Subject: One large dark skillet centred in a three-quarter 45-degree view, filled with sliced seared beef, tender lightly browned button mushrooms, glossy ivory mustard cream sauce, and a small amount of chopped fresh parsley. The beef and mushrooms remain clearly identifiable, with believable appetizing textures and safe margins for a central mobile crop.
Style/medium: polished contemporary manga food illustration, elegant fine ink contours, hand-painted watercolour and soft cel shading, appetizing but believable food textures, a distinctive original CookiGram visual identity; generic manga aesthetic, not imitating any named artist, studio, series or existing artwork.
Composition/framing: landscape 16:9, dish centred with generous safe crop margins for mobile and desktop cards, no human figures.
Lighting/mood: warm late-afternoon kitchen light, welcoming and homely.
Colour palette: ivory cream sauce, warm brown beef and mushrooms, muted green parsley, dark charcoal skillet, warm wood tabletop.
Constraints: visually faithful to the recipe; fully original composition; suitable as a website recipe illustration.
Avoid: text, letters, logos, brand marks, watermarks, product packaging, characters, faces, third-party image imitation, photorealism.
