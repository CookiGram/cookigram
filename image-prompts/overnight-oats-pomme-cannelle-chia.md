# Prompt d’illustration — Overnight oats pomme, cannelle & chia

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: horizontal 16:9 hero illustration for a responsive recipe website
Primary request: Create an entirely original manga-inspired culinary illustration of “Overnight oats pomme, cannelle & chia” based only on the written recipe, not on any existing photograph.
Scene/backdrop: A simple warm home-kitchen tabletop, softly suggested and uncluttered.
Subject: One open glass jar containing thick, creamy overnight oats made with Greek yogurt, visibly fine apple shreds and tiny chia seeds. Show a small optional scatter of chopped walnuts on top, with no other garnish. The apple should look freshly grated and integrated into the pale creamy mixture; cinnamon should read as a subtle warm dusting.
Style/medium: Polished contemporary manga food illustration, elegant fine ink contours, hand-painted watercolour texture and soft cel shading, appetizing but believable food textures, original CookiGram visual identity, generic manga aesthetic and no imitation of any named artist, studio, series or existing artwork.
Composition/framing: Landscape 16:9, three-quarter view at about 45 degrees, jar centred with generous safe crop margins for mobile and desktop cards.
Lighting/mood: Warm natural morning light, welcoming and homely.
Colour palette: Creamy white yogurt and oats, muted red-gold apple shreds, warm cinnamon brown and restrained walnut beige against a quiet amber kitchen background.
Constraints: Visually faithful to the recipe; no milk, no cooked fruit, no berries, no granola, no extra fruit, fully original composition.
Avoid: Text, letters, logos, brand marks, watermarks, product packaging, people, hands, faces, unrelated garnish, third-party image imitation and photorealism.
